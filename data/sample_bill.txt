THE COUNTY HEALTH FACILITIES (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2026

A Bill for an Act of Parliament to amend the County Health Facilities Act, 2018, to
provide for mandatory public participation in the licensing of new private health
facilities, and for connected purposes.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Kenya, as follows —

PART I — PRELIMINARY

1. This Act may be cited as the County Health Facilities (Amendment) Act, 2026.

2. Section 14 of the County Health Facilities Act, 2018 (in this Act referred to as
"the principal Act") is amended by inserting the following new subsection —

   "(3) No licence shall be issued to a private health facility under this section
   unless the applicant has, not less than thirty days before submitting the
   application, published a public notice in at least one newspaper of national
   circulation and posted a notice at the proposed site, inviting members of the
   public within the ward to submit written comments to the County Health
   Licensing Board."

PART II — PUBLIC PARTICIPATION

3. Section 21 of the principal Act is amended by inserting the following new
   subsection —

   "(2A) The County Health Licensing Board shall convene a public participation
   forum within the affected ward not later than sixty days after the close of
   the comment period under section 14(3), and shall publish minutes of that
   forum within fourteen days of its conclusion."

PART III — COMMENCEMENT

4. This Act shall come into force on the date of publication in the Kenya Gazette.

MEMORANDUM OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

The principal object of this Bill is to strengthen community oversight over the
establishment of private health facilities at the ward level, following reports of
private facilities being licensed without adequate consultation with residents
regarding staffing, pricing, and referral pathways to public facilities.

Public participation on this Bill is open for thirty (30) days from the date of
publication. Written comments may be submitted to the Clerk of the National
Assembly, P.O. Box 41842, Nairobi, or via the Parliament of Kenya website.
